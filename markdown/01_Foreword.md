# Foreword

I've known Joel as a student, an instructor, a black belt, and a friend. I've had the opportunity to watch him grow in each of those roles, and one thing has always stood out to me: his desire to understand why things work.

As a student, Joel picked things up quickly, but he was never satisfied with just memorizing techniques. He wanted to understand the details. Why does this grip matter? What makes this position work? What is my opponent trying to accomplish, and how should I respond? I always appreciated that about him because he wasn't just interested in learning more moves. He wanted to develop a deeper understanding of jiu jitsu.

As an instructor, Joel has a natural ability to break down techniques and explain them in a way students can understand. He doesn't just demonstrate a position and tell students what to do. He explains why it works, what details matter, and what both people are trying to accomplish. He wants his students to understand what they're doing rather than simply follow instructions. That's something I respect in any instructor.

I've also watched Joel develop from a white belt into a black belt. Earning a black belt takes years of dedication, but what matters just as much is what you do after receiving it. Joel has never stopped learning. He continues to ask questions, improve his understanding, and find better ways to teach. He genuinely wants to help his students become better practitioners and develop the confidence to make good decisions under pressure.

Outside of jiu jitsu, Joel is someone I'm proud to call a friend. He's loyal, compassionate, thoughtful, and genuinely cares about the people around him. He's always looking for ways to improve himself while helping others do the same. Those qualities carry over into the way he teaches and approaches the art.

This book is a reflection of that approach. Rather than giving readers another collection of techniques to memorize, Joel focuses on understanding the concepts behind them. Posture, grips, head and hip position, alignment, space, control, and leverage are all fundamental parts of jiu jitsu. Understanding how these concepts work together is what allows you to recognize problems, make adjustments, and become a more complete practitioner.

For someone just beginning their journey, this book provides a better way to understand what's happening on the mat. For experienced practitioners and instructors, it offers an opportunity to look at familiar positions from a different perspective and deepen their understanding.

One of the most important things I've learned through years of training and coaching is that knowing a technique and truly understanding it are two different things. The more you understand why something works, the better you'll be able to adapt when things don't go as planned.

Jiu jitsu is a constant learning process, regardless of your experience or rank. Joel understands that, and this book reflects the same curiosity and dedication I've seen in him throughout his journey.

I'm proud of the instructor and black belt Joel has become, and I'm happy to have played a part in his journey.

Ron Keslar
4th Degree Black Belt under Leandro Vieira
Head BJJ and MMA Coach
American Kickboxing Academy San Jose, California
