# Part One: Fundamental Concepts

Part One comes before the positions because its chapters are the vocabulary of this book: the four battles of head height, hip height, grips, and posture and base; quadrants and directional sweeping; the Konami Code; centerline, inside position, and the strike zone; two-on-one control; the process of submission; fulcrums and levers; spinal alignment; yin and yang; dilemmas; and action and reaction. None of them belong to a single position. A concept you can only use in one place is a technique, and it will abandon you the moment the position changes.

Read these chapters for the ideas themselves, not for moves. You will carry each idea into Part Two, where the same problems reappear under the pressure of real positions. When something works or fails on the mat later, the explanation almost always lives here, in a battle that was decided earlier in the exchange.
